GETTING THERE

BY FLIGHT

For guests traveling from abroad, the easiest way to reach San Nicola l'Arena is by flying into Palermo Airport (Falcone Borsellino). The airport is conveniently located about 45 minutes from the village, with several daily flights connecting from Portugal and other major European cities.

BY CAR

Enjoy a scenic drive along the northern coast of Sicily to reach San Nicola l'Arena, a charming seaside village near Palermo. The route offers beautiful coastal views, rolling hills, and glimpses of authentic Sicilian life along the way. If you need to rent a car, we recommend booking with Sicily by Car for a smooth and reliable experience.

ONCE YOU'RE THERE

Take the time to enjoy Sicily's rich culture, delicious cuisine, and stunning landscapes. From vibrant Palermo to charming coastal and hilltop towns, the island is perfect for a week-long getaway around our wedding!

WHERE TO STAY

San Nicola l'Arena offers charming hotels, B&Bs, and villas. For more options, consider staying in Palermo, just 45 minutes away.
